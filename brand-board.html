01 · Colour · guideline §2

Pitch black, white, one blue.

The field is #000000 on every surface. White is the working colour. Electric Blue is the signal: brand, current place, live state, hover, focus — never body text, never a fill at rest. Hot is the action colour only: at most 1 percent of a surface, at most 48 px, black text on it.

02 · Type · guideline §3

Space Grotesk carries display and prose, 300 to 700 — it caps at 700 and has no italics. Space Mono is static 400/700 and carries labels, data, and code. Both self-hosted, SIL OFL 1.1, subsetted WOFF2. Inter and JetBrains Mono are retired to history.

03 · The mark · guideline §5

One colour. The blue dot is retired.

Final geometry, settled 2026-11-20: stem taper 72 to 62, stem height 300, gaps 36 and 36, square 72 by 72, dot diameter 82 with a 5-unit overshoot. White on black is primary; the same geometry in black is the one-colour form for light surfaces. The mark and the wordmark are never adjacent — no lockups exist.

04 · Texture · guideline §1

Two systems. Tone is coverage, never grey.

Dot render carries images and depth: dots grow, ink stays white. The character field carries texture and meaning: glyphs densify, ink stays white. Every animated texture ships with one designed static frame.
